D. DELETION AND RETENTION, NOW SPECIFIED

Deleting a vault removes its guests, its answers sealed and unlocked, its outcome records, its scoring data, and its cover photo from object storage.

Deleting an operator account removes every vault they own under the same cascade, plus their profile and their authentication record.

Billing records are retained and anonymized. Keep the tier, the amount, the date, the currency, the gift code if any, and the Stripe payment reference. Replace the operator name and email with a deleted-account placeholder. The application stores only the Stripe reference, never a copy of Stripe's customer record.

Audit log entries are never deleted. They point at the anonymized operator id.

Prior commits in the backup repository retain deleted data by design, since the repository is version history. This is accepted and deliberate, and it will be disclosed in the privacy policy.

Deletion must be deliberate, never something an operator can fall into
- Deletion is not a single button. It requires an explicit confirmation step that states plainly that deleting the account permanently ends all online access to the vault, the predictions, the results, and the reports, and that this cannot be undone.
- Require the operator to type a confirmation phrase to proceed.
- No deletion control sits inside a routine settings flow next to ordinary saves.

Export offered before deletion, paid tiers only
- Before the final confirmation, a paid operator is offered a downloadable export of their vault data.
- The export contains only content that has already unlocked. Sealed predictions are excluded and are not counted, previewed, or hinted at in the file. Deletion must never become a way to open a vault early.
- Free tier operators are offered no export, consistent with item B.
- Generate the export through the existing unlocked-content path. Do not add a second read path.

Admin full export, the one sanctioned exception
- The admin panel gets an action that produces a complete export of a vault including sealed answers, for owner use in support or legal situations.
- Treat it exactly like manual unlock in the Admin Panel Brief section 4. It requires a fresh MFA re-prompt, a typed reason, and an append-only audit log entry recording the admin identity, the timestamp, the target vault, the reason, and the fact that sealed content was included.
- It is never exposed to operators, guests, or gifters, and it is never linked from any operator-facing screen.

Terms of service and privacy policy will spell out the deletion consequences, the retained anonymized billing record, the backup history, and the admin export. Those documents are written after the build and remain open item 19.9.

- It must fall after the anchor date and after the seal date. Reject anything earlier at validation with a clear message.
- It may fall beyond the ten year plan horizon. The intended use includes cases like the day a child turns 18, so do not clamp it to the plan duration. The plan duration governs the regular schedule only.

Never two separate events on the same day, and never two buttons for the same date on the guest screen. Show one button, labeled as the milestone.
- The milestone is editable until the vault is sealed, then locked, matching the anchor date rule.

The milestone is a guest-pickable timing slot

Guests never enter or name dates. They tap one of the fixed reveal dates the operator's schedule produced. On a Deep Vault, append the operator's milestone date to the end of that list as one more button, for example Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, and then Milestone. Answers assigned to the milestone slot get an unlock_at of the milestone date and unlock as part of the grand finale alongside the grand summary. Scoring is unchanged: the operator enters the true outcome once per question and the software scores every guest from it.
