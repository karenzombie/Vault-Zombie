---
name: Gift resend boundary
description: Defines what “resend” means before transactional email is authorized.
---

Step 8 may retain the gift code, printable card data, and gifter contact needed for a future resend, but it must not add a resend action or invent a non-email delivery channel. Actual resend delivery belongs to Step 9 transactional email.

**Why:** The gift requirements mention admin resend capability while explicitly deferring all gift email delivery. Treating resend as a Step 8 action would either start Step 9 early or invent an unauthorized channel.

**How to apply:** Keep copy and print available in Step 8. Add admin resend only when Step 9 email work is explicitly authorized.
