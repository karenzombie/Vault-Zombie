# Vault Zombie: Email Spec Addendum 1

**Applies to:** `VaultZombie-Email-Spec.md`
**Date:** September 12, 2026

Read both documents. Where this addendum covers something, it governs. Everything else in the email spec is unchanged, including all other copy in section 6.

---

## A1. H2's cover checklist line

Section 6, H2 "Vault created," lists eight setup checklist items. The seventh reads:

> Pick a cover

**Read it now as:**

> Add your own cover photo, if you want one

**It is also conditional.** Unlike the other seven items, this line does not always appear:

- On Safe, Vault, and Deep Vault, the line appears. It shows unchecked until the host has uploaded a cover photo, and checked once they have.
- On Lockbox, the line does not appear in the checklist at all. A Lockbox host cannot upload a photo, so the vault's checklist shows seven items rather than eight.

**Why it changed.** Every vault now receives its vault type's silhouette as its cover automatically, on every tier, from the moment it is created. The old line would therefore show as done the instant the vault existed, which tells the host nothing. Uploading a photo is optional and available only on paid tiers, so the line now names the thing the host can actually choose to do, without implying they owe it.

The checklist's marking rule is unchanged: a completed step shows a check mark in Bronze, an incomplete step shows an empty box in Text 2.

---

## A2. Nothing else in H2 changes

The subject line, eyebrow, main heading, opening paragraph, button, the other seven checklist items, the "Before you seal, remember" block, and the dark band with its stat tiles all stand exactly as written in section 6.

No other email is affected.
