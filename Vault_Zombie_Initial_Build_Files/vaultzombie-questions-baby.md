# VaultZombie Question Bank: New Baby Vault

For a baby shower or new arrival (guests predict the birth and the first year). This type is distinct from Child Growth: it is the short, near-term guessing game people already play at a shower, and it suits the shorter reveal schedules (Weekly Sprint, Monthly x3, Monthly Year) best. The due-date pool lives inside this bank as its first sub-category. Two parents give it a natural "which one will the baby take after" name-pick axis.

## How to read this

- **Three levels:** Vault type (New Baby) > Sub-category (8 below) > Questions.
- **Placeholders:** "the baby" resolves to the baby's name once known (or stays "the baby" at a shower). "Parent A" and "Parent B" resolve to the two parents.
- **Answer type** is fixed per question. Four types:
  - **Number:** guest enters a figure (an age in months, a count, a length in inches).
  - **Name-pick:** guest picks Parent A or Parent B (with "Both" or "A mix" where noted).
  - **Multiple choice:** guest picks from the defined options listed.
  - **Free text:** guest types an answer, hard-capped at 140 characters.
- **No reveal date is attached to a question.** The operator picks one reveal schedule at vault setup (Weekly Sprint, Monthly x3, Monthly Year, Half-then-Annual, or Annual Keepsake), and that schedule produces the vault's reveal dates. When a guest answers, they pick one of those dates for each answer. See the master build brief, section 4.
- All content is editable, reorderable, and expandable from the admin panel without code changes. The "Boy or girl" prompt can be retired in one tap if the family already knows or prefers to skip it.

**Count:** 96 questions across 8 sub-categories (12 each).
