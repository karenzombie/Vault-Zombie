# VaultZombie Question Bank: New Business / Startup Vault

For a business or startup launch (the team, friends, and early backers predict how it will unfold). Good for a launch party, a grand opening, or a founding-team milestone. Single business subject with a founder, so this type leans on number, multiple choice, and free text.

## How to read this

- **Three levels:** Vault type (New Business) > Sub-category (8 below) > Questions.
- **Placeholders:** "[Business]" resolves to the company or brand name at setup. "[Founder]" resolves to the founder's name.
- **Answer type** is fixed per question. Three used here:
  - **Number:** guest enters a figure (a count, a revenue in thousands, a number of years or months).
  - **Multiple choice:** guest picks from the defined options listed.
  - **Free text:** guest types an answer, hard-capped at 140 characters.
  - (Name-pick is not used in this type. It can be added from the admin panel, for example a co-founder axis.)
- **No reveal date is attached to a question.** The operator picks one reveal schedule at vault setup (Weekly Sprint, Monthly x3, Monthly Year, Half-then-Annual, or Annual Keepsake), and that schedule produces the vault's reveal dates. When a guest answers, they pick one of those dates for each answer. See the master build brief, section 4.
- All content is editable, reorderable, and expandable from the admin panel without code changes.

**Count:** 96 questions across 8 sub-categories (12 each).
