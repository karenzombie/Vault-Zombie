How it works

Seal a guess today. Unlock it when it matters.

VaultZombie turns your event into a game that plays out over years. Here is the whole thing, start to finish.

1
Create
Pick a vault type, set your event date, choose how often guesses unlock, and curate your prompts.
2
Share
Print the QR code on your invites or set it out at the venue. Guests scan and seal a guess in about a minute.
3
Reveal
On each date, that batch of guesses opens. You mark what really happened, and the scoreboard updates.
The reveal schedule

You choose the tempo.

At setup you pick one schedule. It sets the exact dates guests choose from, and how the story unspools.

Weekly Sprint

A reveal every week for eight weeks. Built for short, fun events.

Monthly x3

A reveal each month for three months. A quick taste of the whole idea.

Monthly Year

A reveal every month for thirteen months. A full first-year ritual.

Half-then-Annual

One reveal at six months, then every anniversary. A long range with an early payoff.

Annual Keepsake

A reveal every year, up to your plan's limit. The classic long-range life vault.

Anchored to your day

Every reveal date is counted from your event date, so anniversaries land on the anniversary.

Scoring

How the scoreboard adds up.

You enter what really happened once per prompt, and the app scores every guest from it. No marking by hand.

Full

Called it

An exact match. The guess lands dead on.

Half

Close

Inside the range you set. Near-misses still climb the board.

Zero

Nope

A miss. Wild guesses earn nothing.

Just-for-fun keepsake prompts never touch the score. They are there for the memories.

About

Made to be opened later.

VaultZombie started with a simple thought at a friend's wedding: everyone there had an opinion about the years ahead, and none of it was written down. So we built a way to seal those guesses and open them when they finally come true.

Sealed, on purpose

A guess stays locked until its day. Guests cannot peek, the vault owner cannot peek, and dashboards show counts, never the words inside. The whole promise is that a prediction is read on the date it was meant for, and the product is built from the ground up to keep it that way.

Heirloom, not horror

The look is archival and warm, meant to be kept. Our little safe-cracking zombie is here for the fun of it, not to spook anyone. He just really likes opening what was locked away.

Legal

Terms & privacy.

The plain-language version lives here. The full documents are linked at the bottom of every page.

What we store

The less we hold, the less there is to lose. Guests give a guess and an email so we can tell them when it unlocks, and they can opt out of the email. No guest accounts, no addresses, no phone numbers.

Sealed means sealed

Guesses are locked until their reveal date. Guests, vault owners, and gift buyers cannot read sealed content before then. Dashboards show counts, never the words inside.

Your cover photo

If you upload one, we strip location data from it on the way in, resize it, and store it under a random name. It is shown at your event and is never treated as sealed content.

Payments

Handled by Stripe. Card details never touch our servers. Purchases are one-time and gift codes are single use.
