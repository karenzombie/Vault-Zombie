# VaultZombie Question Bank: Child Growth Vault

For a vault about a child growing up (guests predict who this kid will become). The subject is the child; two parents give this type its natural "which one will they take after" name-picks.

## How to read this

- **Three levels:** Vault type (Child Growth) > Sub-category (8 below) > Questions.
- **Placeholders:** "[Child]" resolves to the child's name at setup. "Parent A" and "Parent B" resolve to the two parents (or the two people setting up the vault).
- **Answer type** is fixed per question. Four types:
  - **Number:** guest enters a figure (an age, a height, a count, and so on).
  - **Name-pick:** guest picks Parent A or Parent B (with "Both" or "A mix" added where noted). This is the only two-person axis in a single-child vault.
  - **Multiple choice:** guest picks from the defined options listed.
  - **Free text:** guest types an answer, hard-capped at 140 characters.
- **No reveal date is attached to a question.** The operator picks one reveal schedule at vault setup (Weekly Sprint, Monthly x3, Monthly Year, Half-then-Annual, or Annual Keepsake), and that schedule produces the vault's reveal dates. When a guest answers, they pick one of those dates for each answer. See the master build brief, section 4.
- All content is editable, reorderable, and expandable from the admin panel without code changes.

**Count:** 104 questions across 8 sub-categories (13 each).
