## B1. The Lockbox cover section

Section 4.1 states that on Lockbox the upload control does not appear at all, with no upsell or placeholder. That is amended.

**A Lockbox host sees their vault type's silhouette, and beneath it one line of quiet text:**

> Upgrade to any paid plan to use your own photo.

**How it is presented:**

- Plain body text in Text 2 `#55514A`, at the small body size.
- Not a button, not a link, not a banner, and not in an accent color.
- No upload input and no remove control anywhere in the section.

**What does not change:** every vault on every tier still carries its type's silhouette as its cover, assigned automatically. The host never picks from the library. Photo upload remains available on Safe, Vault, and Deep Vault only, and everything in 4.1 about upload handling stands as written.

**Why it changed.** The original rule kept the setup screen clear of selling. In practice a Lockbox host who sees only a silhouette has no way of knowing a photo is possible at all. One quiet line tells them what is available without turning the screen into a pitch.
