Contrast note: keep body text in Ink or Text 2. Bronze is safe for large text, icons, and borders, but sits below AA for small body copy on white, so avoid it for paragraphs.

Usage rules
✓Save Tilt Warp for moments. Wordmark, hero, and the reveal. Never a functional heading or anything guests read fast.
✓Let the foundation lead. Most of any screen is Ink text on Parchment or White. Accents stay rare.
✓One primary action per view. A single Ink button, everything else secondary.
✕Don't set body copy in bronze. It reads as accent and fails contrast at small sizes.
✕Don't stack Tilt Warp lines. Two warped headings together get noisy and hurt legibility.
✕Don't mix in cool grays. Keep neutrals warm so they sit with the parchment and bronze.
